Orbi

A free social app for sharing what moves you.

Orbi lets you share short posts, photos, reels and stories with people anywhere in the world, follow the people you like, and chat with them.

Open Orbi

What you can do on Orbi

  • Orbs: write posts with photos or video, add polls, reply and quote.
  • Orbit: follow people to fill your Home with their orbs, reels and stories.
  • Re-orb and like: share what you enjoy and show support.
  • Reels: watch and post short vertical videos, with music.
  • Stories: share moments that disappear after a day, or only with Close Friends.
  • Chat: send private messages to people you connect with.

Signing in

You can create an Orbi account with your email, or with Google. When you choose "Continue with Google", Orbi only receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google, to create your account and show your name on your profile. Orbi does not read your Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

Your data and your safety. Read how we handle your information in the Privacy Policy, the rules for using Orbi in the Terms of Service, and what is and is not allowed in the Community Guidelines.
