Community Guidelines

Last updated 24 October 2026

Make Orbi a good place

Orbi is for everyone. These guidelines help keep it safe and friendly.

Treat people with respect

No harassment, bullying, threats, or hate against people because of who they are, including their race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, or nationality.

Keep people safe

No violence, no encouraging self-harm, and no sharing of private information about others. Never post sexual content involving minors; we remove it and report it where the law requires.

Be real

No impersonation, scams, fake engagement, or spam. Do not pretend to be someone else.

Sensitive content

If a photo or video is graphic or adult, mark it as sensitive so people can choose whether to open it. Some content is not allowed even when marked.

Respect creators

Only post photos, videos and music you have the right to use. Give credit where it is due.

Reports and consequences

Use the menu on any person or content to report it. Rule breaking can lead to removal of content, limits on features, or account suspension.
