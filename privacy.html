Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

The short version

We collect what we need to run Orbi, we do not sell your personal information, and you can control or delete your data.

What we collect

Account details: your email address or sign-in provider (such as Google), username, display name and password (stored securely). Profile details you choose to add: photo, banner, bio, location and website. Content: orbs, reels, stories, comments, messages, likes, orbits, bookmarks and the photos, videos and audio you upload. Invites: if you join with an invite link, we record who invited you. Usage: basic events such as when you sign up, log in, post or view content, and your device and browser type, so we can fix problems and understand how Orbi is used. Location: only if you choose to add a location sticker, in which case your device asks permission first.

How we use it

To run Orbi: to show your profile and content, deliver your messages and alerts, keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, send account emails (such as password resets), and improve the app. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show you ads based on it.

What others can see

Your profile, orbs, reels and stories are visible to other Orbi users (stories shared with Close Friends are visible only to your Close Friends list). Messages are visible only to you and the person you message. Orbi does not currently use end-to-end encryption, so do not share secrets in chats.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run Orbi: our hosting and database provider (Supabase), our email sending provider, and your sign-in provider if you use one. They may only use your data to provide their service to us. We may also share information if the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile and content are removed. Some copies may remain in backups for a short time, and content others have already shared may remain with them.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, delete your content, block or mute accounts, choose who can message you, download your data, and delete your account in Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights, such as asking for access to or correction of your data. Contact us and we will help.

Browser storage

Orbi stores a small amount of data in your browser: your sign-in session, your light or dark mode choice, and a cache so the app opens quickly. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Children

Orbi is not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child has joined, report the account and we will remove it.

Security

We use access rules and encryption in transit to protect your data, but no service can promise perfect security. Use a strong, unique password.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Contact us using the support details in Settings, under Help center.
